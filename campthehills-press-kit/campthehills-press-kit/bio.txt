MEDIUM
campthehills is a self-producing independent artist and composer from
Missoula, Montana, now based in Los Angeles. He writes, produces and
paints his own cover art, and released the single "bray fool" in 2026
alongside "we can't behave", "down with the snow" and a self-titled EP.
He began producing for other artists under the name Spaceman, with
credits including $uicideboy$, Toosii & Summer Walker, Polo G, Yeat and
Lil Tecca.
